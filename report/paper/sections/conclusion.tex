\section{Conclusion}
The completed experiments show why depth evaluation needs explicit representations and missing-output accounting. StereoSGBM's modest conditional RMSE coexists with substantial missing coverage. On NYUv2, the tested UniDepthV2 ViT-S configuration provides stronger direct metric accuracy than DA V2-S Hypersim, while GT-aligned relative results answer a different question and include a retained failure. Boundary metrics reveal distinctions obscured by a single global error.

A causal flow-guided fusion system, six ablations, common temporal evaluation, and a point-depth application are implemented and numerically checked. Their real-video benefit remains unmeasured until independent self-recorded clips are supplied. Completion requires development-only selection, frozen testing, categorized failures, and member acceptance. Camera-coordinate changes and imperfect flow remain limitations; reducing agreement error alone will not establish improved physical accuracy.
