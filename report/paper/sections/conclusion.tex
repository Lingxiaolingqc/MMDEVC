\section{Conclusion}
StereoSGBM attains 1.3113-pixel scene-average disparity MAE at only 62.65\% pooled valid coverage, while UniDepthV2 ViT-S attains 0.1108 AbsRel on all 654 NYUv2 test images under the revised protocol. Our single improvement, relative flow-guided fusion, reduces the temporal-change proxy by 15.9\% on the complete 70-frame example and avoids severe same-pixel EMA trails. CPU flow overhead lowers estimated throughput to 3.10 fps, and fine-detail failures remain.

These results establish a reproducible smoothing trade-off, not higher metric accuracy or universal generalization. The short test scene, absence of video GT/static controls, uncertain flow, and relative-scale ambiguity limit the conclusion. More independent sequences with depth and pose references would allow accuracy and motion response to be tested separately.
