\begin{abstract}
We study pretrained small-model depth estimation through calibrated stereo, single-image metric evaluation, and relative video prediction. On 15 Middlebury training pairs, StereoSGBM achieves a scene-average disparity MAE of 1.3113 pixels on valid predictions, with only 62.65\% pixel-pooled coverage. UniDepthV2 ViT-S achieves 0.1108 AbsRel, 0.3928 m RMSE, and 0.8948 $\delta_1$ on the standard 654-image NYUv2 test split under the revised course crop and clipping protocol. Our one improvement is causal flow-guided fusion of Depth Anything V2 relative outputs. We select its history weight on a separate 100-frame development clip and freeze it before evaluating the complete 70-frame official rollercoaster example. Fusion reduces a normalized temporal-change proxy by 15.9\% on this example while retaining sharper moving contours than same-pixel averaging, but adds substantial CPU flow cost and does not establish improved depth accuracy. We compare all six ablations, preserve raw outputs and timestamps, and demonstrate relative depth-based background blur. The report documents the official source's 70-frame length versus the requested 100 frames. Code: \url{https://github.com/Lingxiaolingqc/MMDEVC}.
\end{abstract}
