\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Geometry and benchmarks.}
Semi-Global Matching (SGM) uses pathwise cost aggregation for stereo correspondence~\cite{sgm}. OpenCV StereoSGBM is a modified implementation~\cite{opencv}, not an exact replication of every original component. Middlebury supplies calibrated stereo and disparity GT~\cite{middlebury}; KITTI provides driving-scene geometric benchmarks~\cite{kitti}. We use Middlebury, while KITTI motivates attention to dataset scope.

\paragraph{Monocular foundations.}
Dense Prediction Transformers reassemble transformer features for dense prediction~\cite{dpt}. Depth Anything emphasizes large-scale unlabeled-image supervision~\cite{dav1}. Depth Anything V2 uses synthetic teacher supervision and pseudo-labeled real images to train efficient students, with separate relative and metric releases~\cite{dav2}. Our video experiment uses relative outputs, which remain distinct from metre-valued predictions.

\paragraph{Metric depth.}
UniDepth conditions metric 3D prediction on a learned camera representation~\cite{unidepth}. UniDepthV2 adds edge-oriented training, a simplified architecture, and uncertainty output~\cite{unidepthv2}. Our ViT-S run uses predicted intrinsics and returned Z depth; its uncertainty is not used in fusion. Depth Pro combines multi-scale prediction, sharp boundaries, and focal-length estimation~\cite{depthpro}. It motivates boundary inspection but is not executed here.

\paragraph{Motion and video.}
RAFT estimates optical flow with all-pairs correlations and recurrent refinement~\cite{raft}. We use Farneb\"ack's local polynomial method~\cite{farneback}, accepting limitations at reflections, weak texture, and large motion. VDA adds a spatial-temporal head and temporal training objectives to the DA V2 foundation~\cite{vda}. Its official offline inference differs from our causal postprocessor, so throughput does not imply equivalent online latency. Our adaptive weights are a transparent heuristic, not calibrated probabilities.
